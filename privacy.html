On-device data

Birth details, readings, favorites and journal entries are processed and saved on your device. Calculations work offline; purchases, paired Apple Watch transfers, sharing and external links can use device connections or online services.

The birth date, time and place you enter, including coordinates from the built-in city list, are used for local chart calculations. Readings, favorites and journal entries are kept in the app's local storage, not on a developer server.

Purchases handled by Apple

Apple's StoreKit handles product and price loading, purchases, purchase verification and restores. Zodira receives product information and verified access and transaction results; Apple may process your Apple Account, payment and transaction data under its privacy policy. Apple sets the retention rules for its commerce records. This is Apple-managed processing, not a Zodira account or payment backend.

Your paired Apple Watch

WatchConnectivity sends a minimal journal snapshot to your paired Apple Watch, not to a developer server. It contains one record identifier, a normalized next action of up to 180 characters, an optional outcome and synchronization metadata. It excludes the birth profile and the full question, context, options, evidence, result and learning.

Technical names of the transmitted fields

schemaVersion · sourceID · revision · operation · recordID · nextAction · nextActionWasCondensed · outcome · recordUpdatedAt · issuedAt

The Watch caches the latest validated update. New updates replace the previous snapshot. When the synchronized journal content is cleared, a clear message (tombstone) replaces that cached content after the Watch receives and accepts it. Clearing is not necessarily immediate: a disconnected Watch can retain an earlier copy.

Sharing you choose

Using ShareLink sends the selected journal record to the recipient, app or service you choose in the system share sheet. The shared text is the complete record in JSON, not the minimal Watch snapshot; if encoding fails, only the question is shared. Recipients and services retain their own copies under their rules. Local deletion does not recall those copies.

Storage and deletion

Use the app's deletion controls to remove saved readings or journal data. Deleting Zodira removes its local app container, but does not guarantee deletion of Keychain entries, Watch cache, Apple backups or copies shared with others. Their separate retention rules are described in this policy.

To remember successful feature-trial use, a separate Keychain ledger stores operationID, feature and consumedAt, with a local mirror. It contains trial-use records, not journal contents, and is not sent to us. Keychain is outside the app container and can survive deleting or reinstalling the app; deleting journal data or the app does not guarantee erasing this ledger.

Apple and device settings determine whether app data is included in backups. Existing backups are not erased when local app data is deleted. Use Apple's backup controls to manage or delete these copies; their retention is governed by Apple and your settings, not by Zodira's developer.

External links and websites

Opening support, privacy, EULA, review or other links connects to external websites. Their hosting services receive normal request data, such as IP addresses and browser information, under their own policies. This site uses GitHub Pages and loads app images from Apple. These web requests are separate from Zodira's local app calculations.
